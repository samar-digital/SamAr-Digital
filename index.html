Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few basics about the product vision and this early landing page.

Is BizFlow available to use right now?

This is an early landing-page concept. The actual CRM, accounts, database, and automation features still need to be built and tested.

Can I import existing invoices and orders?

Importing existing records is part of the planned workflow. Supported formats and import limits will be confirmed once that feature is implemented.

Will it work through WhatsApp?

WhatsApp integration is planned, subject to the official platform setup, approved permissions, and secure account verification. The example conversation shown here is illustrative only.

Will my business data be private?

Privacy and tenant-level data isolation must be built into the actual product before real business records are stored. Do not upload confidential records to this demo page.

How can I get updates?

Use one of the early-access buttons to register interest in the concept. This prototype does not yet submit or store your details.
